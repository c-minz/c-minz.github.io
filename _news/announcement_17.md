The paper extending our numerical approximation scheme for modular Hamiltonians to fermionic theories has now been published: ['Numerical approach to the modular operator for fermionic systems'](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11005-026-02175-4).

Earlier this month, also the conference proceedings for the The Seventeenth Marcel Grossmann Meeting from 2024 came out, which include a short article summarising the results from my conference contribution: ['Do causal sets have symmetries?'](https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/10.1142/9789819839926_0169).
